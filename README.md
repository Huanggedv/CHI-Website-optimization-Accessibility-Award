# CHI Website Optimization — Accessibility & Awards

A redesign concept for two pages of the CHI 2026 conference program site (programs.sigchi.org): the **Accessibility FAQ** and the **Awards** page.

Part of a team project under the HMW: *How might we make complex conference information easier to understand and explore for attendees with different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience?*

## Pages

- `accessibility.html` — FAQ grouped by need, with a status on every answer (Available / Request / Not offered / Pending), request timeline and venue facilities by floor.
- `awards.html` — 62 Best Papers browsable by topic and by day, sessions with multiple Best Papers, and an "Add to my day" list.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or view through GitHub Pages. No build step; `dc-lite.js` renders the page templates.

## Data

Content is based on the CHI 2026 Accessibility FAQ and the CHI 2026 Best Paper list. Times and rooms come from the CHI 2026 programme data. Topic groups and the short plain-language summaries are drafts made for this concept.
